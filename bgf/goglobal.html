Go Global

The Model: €60 A Lifetime of Change

Each €60 donation package includes:
  • 2 fruit/cashew trees for farmers.
  • 2 sustainability trees (Melia volkensii) managed by BGFF.
  • €5 for education.
  • €5 for microfinance.
After 15 years, without new donations:
  • 4 fruit trees donated.
  • 4 Melia trees replanted.
  • €10 in education.
  • €10 in microfinance.
Your impact doubles. And the cycle continues.
